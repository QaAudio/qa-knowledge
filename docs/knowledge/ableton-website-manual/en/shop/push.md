### Am I eligible for an educational discount?

You are eligible for a discounted Live license if you are:

*   currently enrolled in full-time educational studies (up to advanced degree programs), and are 18+ years old or of legal age in your country of residence. Parents of a student who is not of legal age can buy an educational license on behalf of their child.
*   a student in any part-time program that consists of 20 hours or more of study time per week, or one that runs for 12 months or more.
*   a teacher who is currently employed full- or part-time at a school or university in any field. Part-time and private teachers must work at least 20 hours per week to qualify.

Please note: you’ll need to be able to prove that you are a student or teacher to authorize your license – [see acceptable forms of proof](https://help.ableton.com/hc/en-us/articles/206431524-Eligibility-requirements-for-educational-licenses). Educational discounts can’t be combined with upgrade pricing.

## Need help?

### Is Push available in my country?

Compliance policies for electronic devices vary from region to region; Push is available from ableton.com in Australia, Canada, the European Union, China, Iceland, Japan, Liechtenstein, New Zealand, Norway, South Korea, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and the United States of America. In countries where we don’t have compliance, retail partners may be able to sell Push, since they take care of compliance management in their country. 

Learn more about how this relates to [Push orders in your country](https://www.ableton.com/en/buying-push-and-move/).

### Can I upgrade my Push 2 to use it as a standalone instrument?

No, it’s not possible to upgrade your Push 2.

### What is the Upgrade Kit?

The Upgrade Kit allows you to upgrade your Push to the Standalone version. With the standalone components installed, your Push will be exactly the same as a Push bought with a processor.

### How do I install the Upgrade Kit?

The Upgrade Kit includes all the parts and tools you need to upgrade your Push at home. Please follow the [step-by-step guide](https://www.ableton.com/en/push-upgrade-guide/).

### Where can I purchase the Upgrade Kit?

The Upgrade Kit can be purchased in our [webshop.](https://www.ableton.com/en/shop/merchandise/)

### What version of Live do I need to use Push?

You’ll need Live 11.3 or later to use Push 3. Older versions of Live won’t work with your new Push.

### What is the refund policy?

If you decide Push isn’t for you, you can return it within 30 days for a refund.

### Which shipping carrier is used?

*   UK (except Northern Ireland): We ship all orders from our warehousing partner in the UK.

There are various payment options available. You can find all the accepted payment methods in your country [in this help article](https://help.ableton.com/hc/en-us/articles/206301044-Accepted-payment-methods).
