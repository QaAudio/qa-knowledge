## Ableton Meetup Tokyo

Ableton Meetup Tokyo is Tokyo-based Ableton user community which aims to make a sustainable musical life for all musicians.

## Almada - Lisbon

O Ableton Users Group ~ Lisboa é uma iniciativa que convida todos os utilizadores do Ableton Live, principiantes ou profissionais, residentes ou visitantes, à reunião para a partilha de ideias, técnicas de produção ou ferramentas úteis à utilização do Live, tudo no sentido de melhorar o workflow e aperfeiçoar o domínio sobre o programa.   
Já que a utilização deste consiste numa experiência mais individualista, convidamos à aproximação de todos para que as nossas ideias nos influenciem uns aos outros e, com isso, a nossa experiência com o Live se torne mais rica e completa!   
Todos são bem-vindos, pois acreditamos que a chave se encontra na partilha de pequenas ou grandes ideias que, no final, fazem toda a diferença.  
  
Convidamo-vos, então, a juntarem-se a nós nas reuniões, que surgirão no Desterro, de forma regular, em datas a anunciar. Tragam as vossas técnicas de produção próprias, os pequenos segredos do Live que quiserem partilhar nestas reuniões, o vosso gear, enfim, tudo aquilo que acharem que melhora a vossa experiência com o Live.   
Partilhar é aprender.

## Berlin

The Ableton User Group Berlin is for all interested Ableton Live users based in (or visiting) Berlin. All levels are welcome. The meetings are in both German and English.

The aim of the Group is to help Live users network and share information and experience in an informal environment with each other. The meetings are to allow all participants to suggest discussion themes related to Ableton Live, as well as to provide a forum for presenting their own work.

We also have artist meetings and expert talks which aim to give an insight into how Ableton Live is used in different production and performance contexts, providing insider tips for optimizing workflows. User Group participants are encouraged to present their own work at the meetings with a live or DJ set at the end of the meetings. All Ableton Live users are invited to provide ideas and suggestions for discussions.

The meetups take place monthly at [Panke](http://www.pankecultu­re.com/) in Berlin-Wedding, Gerichtstraße 23, 2nd Building (2.HH). For actual dates please visit our FB Page or Meetup.com page:

## Victoria

The Victoria Ableton User Group connects the community of Live users in Victoria and on Vancouver Island.  All skill levels are welcome.  We cover a wide range of topics, mostly focused on electronic music production and performance.  Some of things we chat about are sound design, synthesis, workflow tips, new features, DJing, mixing, mastering and remixing.  Beyond that, we also discuss broader topics, such as building a project studio, reviews of gear & plugins, music theory, the creative process, the music business and how to make a career of being an artist.  For this reason, we also welcome users of other software and any interested, aspiring musicians.  

We’re hosted by Warp Academy, an Ableton Certified Training Center, located at the McTavish Academy of Arts, 1720 McTavish Road, North Saanich, BC.  This gives us access to a classroom, projector, studio facilities and reference monitors.  Our studio is set up for live streaming, and we’ll be broadcasting and recording some of our larger sessions so members can attend remotely or watch archives.  

As a member, you can learn in a variety of ways.  Our in-person events are lead by the Ableton Certified Trainers at Warp Academy.  Where possible, we also invite touring artists and experienced Live users from the community as guest presenters.  

In addition to the live events, we also publish free tutorial videos every week.  This is a great way to get up to speed fast, or to stay inspired in between sessions.
