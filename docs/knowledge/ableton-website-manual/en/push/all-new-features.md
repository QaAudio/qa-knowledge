## Latest additions

### XYZ Layout

Shape sound in three dimensions. The new XYZ layout lets you control multiple Rack Macros at once by sliding your finger across Push’s expressive pads, adding pressure for extra intensity.

### Rhythm Generator

Build beats that evolve as you work. Rhythm Generator introduces a fast, intuitive way to create drum patterns using four simple sliders for length, density, variation, and shift. 

### Expressive Step Editing

Add feel directly in the sequence. Expressive Step Editing lets you adjust note velocity by touch, sliding across steps to sculpt dynamics with precision or draw smooth curves in a single gesture. 

### Stem Separation

Rework audio with freedom. Stem Separation makes it easy to split a track into vocals, bass, drums, and other elements right from Push. 

### External Audio Interface

Expand your setup, stay standalone. Push now supports class-compliant external audio interfaces in Standalone mode, giving you more inputs and outputs, access to external preamps, and flexible routing for studio or stage.
