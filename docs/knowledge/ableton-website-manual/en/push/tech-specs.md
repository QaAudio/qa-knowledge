## Need help?

To learn more about features and functions watch the [learn Push videos](https://www.ableton.com/en/push/learn-push/) or [read the manual](https://www.ableton.com/push/manual). For troubleshooting tips visit the [knowledge base](https://help.ableton.com/hc/en-us/categories/201129525-Push-).

[My Push is configured for standalone use. Can I also use it to control Live on my computer?](#collapse_3717fc14-d88a-455a-834b-990a016c54ba_1)

Yes, you can connect either configuration of Push to your computer to control Live. Learn about Control Mode in the [Push Manual](https://www.ableton.com/en/push/manual/).

[Can I work in Arrangement View when my Push is in Standalone Mode?](#collapse_3717fc14-d88a-455a-834b-990a016c54ba_2)

Push is designed around Session View. Arrangement View isn’t currently supported on Push in Standalone Mode. Live Sets can be loaded and played back, but the Arrangement timeline can’t be edited on Push in Standalone Mode.

[What are the tech specs of Push’s audio interface?](#collapse_3717fc14-d88a-455a-834b-990a016c54ba_3)

You can find detailed tech specs for Push’s built-in audio interface in this [Help Center article](https://help.ableton.com/hc/articles/8483423483036).

[Will all my Max for Live devices work on Push in Standalone Mode?](#collapse_3717fc14-d88a-455a-834b-990a016c54ba_4)

Most Ableton-built Max for Live devices will work on your Push in Standalone Mode, as will many third-party devices. However, there are a few exceptions. You can find the details in this [Help Center article](https://help.ableton.com/hc/articles/8506527153308).  
  
Please note that translating objects for Push is very complex, so we can’t guarantee that all Max for Live devices (including your self-built patches) will work as expected.

[What instruments and effects come with the standalone configuration of Push?](#collapse_3717fc14-d88a-455a-834b-990a016c54ba_5)

If you buy your Push with a processor it’ll come with all the Ableton Live Intro instruments and effects pre-installed for standalone use. You can see a list of included devices [here](https://www.ableton.com/en/shop/live/). If you own a Live 12 Standard or Live 12 Suite license already, or buy one with your Push, you can use the instruments and effects belonging to your license.

[Will I be able to use my favorite VSTs on Push in Standalone Mode?](#collapse_3717fc14-d88a-455a-834b-990a016c54ba_6)

Push doesn’t support VSTs or Audio Unit plug-ins for use in Standalone Mode. To transfer a Live Set containing plug-ins from your computer to your Push, first freeze any tracks containing plug-ins. Learn more about [freezing tracks](https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/computer-audio-resources-and-strategies/#track-freeze).
