Watch and learn Matt Wilde’s process for making a nu-jazz track in Live: from shaking up drum patterns, to layering recordings and finding the right balance of creative effects for the arrangement. Matt also shares inspiring composition resources like chord diagrams, melodic techniques, and the method he turns to when building out B-sections: resampling.
