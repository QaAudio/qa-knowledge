## The Ensoniq sound: digital character and analog crunch

In the 1980s and 1990s, Ensoniq became known for developing cutting-edge equipment at affordable prices. Their gear had a lasting impact on the musical landscape of the time, helping to popularize the plucky arpeggios, glassy keys, and choir pads that define the era. Ensoniq hardware even ended up in the studios of artists like Vangelis and Jean-Michel Jarre, and is still sought after today for the rich, warm sound produced by its combination of digital chips and analog filtering. 

Anyone interested in genres like synth pop, new wave, electro, and synth funk – or any of the contemporary sounds that are influenced by them – will find plenty to love in Nostalgique. This collection of instruments and drums is taken from three classic pieces of Ensoniq gear:

**Ensoniq Mirage** – a synth/sampler workstation featuring an 8-bit digital sampling engine with 32 kHz resolution and analog filters.

**Ensoniq ESQ-1** – a digital synth that combines an Ensoniq-designed wavetable chip with analog filters and a hybrid 8-voice architecture.

**Ensoniq ASR-X** – a wave-sampling workstation known for its distinctive sequencer.

## Play vintage emulations and explore retro-futuristic ideas

Nostalgique comes with a wide range of synth presets and drum kits to discover. Each preset has been multi-sampled from original Ensoniq hardware and built into an Instrument or Drum Rack – all with multiple variations to choose from and a range of parameters to tweak.

The Pack also includes plenty of one-shot samples and a wide selection of MIDI clips containing melodic and rhythmic patterns. Create perfect vintage emulations using the included presets or rework the source material to experiment with your own new sound.
