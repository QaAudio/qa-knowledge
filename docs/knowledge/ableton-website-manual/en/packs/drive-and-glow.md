# Drive and Glow

### by [Ableton](/en/packs/by/ableton/)

Drive and Glow combines swirling synth textures, overdriven guitars and basses and pounding drums. Carefully curated by Ableton, this Pack is perfect for creating tracks that radiate with saturated, indie vibes.

Developed by Ableton in collaboration with sound designers including Michele Darling, Francis Preve and Sean Charles, this curated collection puts everything you need to create tracks in one place. Drum Racks are packed with classic and modern drum sounds, effect hits, and chords, letting you craft your songs without interruption to your creativity. A wide selection of Instrument Racks delivers classic synth-pop basslines, dreamy pads, keys and more.

Also included are over 300 audio loops, chords and MIDI clips ranging from guitars, basses and keys over percussion to full drum grooves carefully selected to spark your imagination, kickstart your tracks, and add life and detail to your production.  

## Designed for flow

The Pack is set up for sparking ideas and keeping you in your flow. Eight Macros are carefully set up for creative tweaking and sound design for each instrument—especially from Push. Use the effects chains in the included Drum kits and Instrument Racks to turn your low end from big and boomy to focused and tight, and much more.

## Updated for Live 11

Drive and Glow has been refreshed with 185 new MIDI clips, including a new Tonal category featuring basslines, melodies, chord progressions and more.
