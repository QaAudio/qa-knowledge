## Skeleton keys

Electric piano sounds can be found in almost any genre from jazz, blues and soul to pop, funk and hip hop. Electric Keyboards is a focused, playable toolkit of highly-detailed e-pianos for songwriting and improvisation. Each instrument comes with a standard preset showcasing its dry sound, and a small number of versatile effected presets for more out-of-the-ordinary textures. Every sound in this kit places usability above all else, so any project that calls for electric keys will find what it needs here.

## Updated for Live 11

The Pack now contains 120 MIDI clips expressively performed by professional players. A range of chord progressions, vamps and melodic phrases bring these classic keyboard sounds to life. 

## Pack contents

*   Suitcase Piano: three basic presets, recorded via the internal speakers, an external amp, and directly into a high-end preamp
*   Wurly Piano: three basic presets, recorded via the internal speakers, an external amp, and directly into a high-end preamp
*   Tonewheel Organ: one basic preset, recorded via the internal speaker
*   25 additional presets
*   120 MIDI clips including chord progressions, ostinati, vamps and melodic phrases
*   Samples provided by [Jeremy Toy](www.leonardcharlesrecords.com) and [Detunized](https://www.detunized.com/)
