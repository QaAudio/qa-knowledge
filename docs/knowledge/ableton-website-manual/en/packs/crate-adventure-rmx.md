## **Bring the noise**

The direct follow-up to Nu-Mark’s Creme de la Crate Pack, RMX includes Ableton-exclusive samples, presets and MIDI clips that range from melodic loops and catchy instrumental patterns to hard-hitting drum one shots. RMX also comes with a large collection of “Song Starters” – carefully-recorded musical samples from a wide variety of sound sources designed to serve as the foundation for new tracks. From dirty guitar riffs to orchestral swells, no two Song Starters are alike and each one offers a fresh way to get your tracks rolling. You’ll also find a huge collection of vocal hits and phrases in the “Vocal Grunts” section, ideal for adding extra impact to your music and accentuating parts of your production.

Other features include the Latin Funk Guitar Loops, which capture the unique instrumentation and authentic textures of the instrument; Jazz/Funk Drums, whose rusty tones are reminiscent of the 1968-74 era; and Key Loops, which provides dark melodies and colorful lo-fi character.
