## In the loop

Vibrations contains a kaleidoscopic collection of loops – a mix and match kit perfect for kickstarting ideas or adding extra flavor to productions, whether you’re making downtempo electronica, lo-fi hip hop, indie rock, or club tracks. 

The [ModeAudio](http://www.modeaudio.com) team is known for vibrant synth sounds, and plenty have found their way into this Pack, from classic 80s leads and squelchy basslines to glitchy granular slices and pointillistic arpeggios. It’s not all about the synths, though – have fun digging through assorted guitar loops, piano parts, and textures from nature.

## Feel the beat

Beatmakers keeping an ear out for idiosyncratic hits, take note. With 10 Drum Racks and over 200 one shot samples, build beats from classic drum machines and acoustic kits, as well as less-conventional sources like toy percussion and guitar pedals. The loops section also provides plenty of drum grooves and auxiliary percussion recordings to chop and drop into your tunes.

## Play, shape and tweak

Vibrations also features a selection of Wavetable, Analog and Operator-based Instrument Racks – growling bass, bouncing mallets, humming leads and more, all designed especially for this Pack. Finally, give your sounds extra flare with seven Effects Racks for mix, modulation and space.
