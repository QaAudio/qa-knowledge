This realistic multisampled instrument gives you the versatility of an upright piano in Live. Explore the full capabilities of the instrument with Macros for natural and unnatural tweaks – play with pedal and hammer noise, adjust the tone to suit your sonic environment, or slow the attack to turn a piano into a pad. 

The Pack also includes custom Max for Live devices that control the various sample sets individually for added realism, giving you the rich and immersive sound of resonating strings when the sustain pedal is pressed, as well as authentic-sounding hammer and pedal noises that occur naturally while you play.  

A selection of MIDI clips in different melodic styles and chord progressions can provide inspiration, start you on a creative path, or serve as the basis for experimentation.

Watch demo videos and find in-depth manuals for the Upright Piano, Brass Quartet and String Quartet Packs on the [Spitfire Audio website](http://www.spitfireaudio.com/ableton).

## **Spitfire Audio**

Spitfire Audio is a world leader in instrumental multisampling. The company, which started as a collection of string samples recorded for the founders’ own compositions, has released over 60 sample libraries which can be heard in major Hollywood film scores and hit records.
