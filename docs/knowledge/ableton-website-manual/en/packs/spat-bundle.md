The SPAT Devices bundle is a collection of advanced audio spatialization tools by Music Unit that offer in-depth control over the placement and behavior of sounds in real and imagined three-dimensional spaces. In development for over thirty years, the technology behind the devices in both Packs is capable of real-time stereo, binaural and transaural processing, while the SPAT Multichannel Pack goes even further with the ability to control up to 32 satellite speakers in dozens of configurations.

## SPAT Multichannel

SPAT Multichannel is for artists, producers and sound engineers who want to get the most out of their studio or live venue’s multichannel setup. It comes with all of the key devices that can be found in the Stereo version, but with extra controls on each device for programming up to 32 speakers – as well as an additional Speaker Editor tool that lets you replicate your real-world setup in Live.

For more details, go to the [SPAT Multichannel Pack page](https://www.ableton.com/en/packs/spat-multichannel/).

## SPAT Stereo

The SPAT Stereo Pack is for those with straightforward stereo and headphone setups who still want to integrate quality spatialization techniques in their productions – whether it’s creating binaural sounds with headphones or using the transaural panning algorithm with studio monitors. The Pack includes stereo versions of all of the core devices that make the SPAT technology so powerful: Spatial, Room and Multiverb.

For more details, go to the [SPAT Stereo Pack page.](https://www.ableton.com/en/packs/spat-stereo/)
