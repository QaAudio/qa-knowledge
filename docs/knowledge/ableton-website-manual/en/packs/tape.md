Analog tape has a smoothing effect and adds weight and glue to a mix, helping to turn a recording from a collection of individual tracks into a cohesive and compelling piece of music.The Softube Tape Pack, with its component-level and physical modeling of three different tape machines, gives you all the vintage warmth you could want in one place. 

## Sweet and simple

Select your favorite tape machine (A, B, or C) and adjust the Amount knob, or simply load up one of the included presets to soak your tracks in analog tape character. Tape is CPU-efficient. Use it on your main track, or do it the retro way by adding an instance to every track. Tons of detail and texture, no CPU overload.

## Choose your flavor

Type A is based on a classic Swiss high end reel-to-reel machine, known and loved for its precision and linearity. Type B is much more colorful – a transformer-based machine which adds extra weight and cream to the low end. Lastly, type C is based on a British tape machine with a distinct vintage vibe.

## Summary

*   True analog tape sound
*   Three tape machine types included
*   Easy to use
*   Low CPU usage
*   Wobble control to introduce wow and flutter.
