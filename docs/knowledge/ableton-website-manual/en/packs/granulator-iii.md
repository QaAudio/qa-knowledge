Like its predecessors, Granulator III allows users to explore the creative potential of granular synthesis — looping and modulating short snippets of sound to create new material. The technique lends itself to exploring pads and textures, but this significant update for Live 12 has widened the scope of Henke’s device with deeper functionality and a more intuitive interface. 

As well as working with samples in the usual way, Granulator III can capture real-time audio. You can now choose from three granular playback modes — Classic Mode which functions just like Granulator II, Loop Mode to work with and create rhythmic content and Cloud Mode for drones and experimental textures. You can also use an MPE-enabled controller in Granulator III to control parameters such as pitch bends, grain position and size on a per-note basis, allowing for richer expression when playing your uniquely sculpted sounds. 

All these features and more are loaded into an interface which has been re-designed to be as approachable as possible, putting cinematic soundscapes and evolving sound design within easy reach. If you want a starting point, the Pack also comes bundled with a selection of samples particularly suited to granular synthesis, as well as presets and device templates. Whether you enjoy mutating samples into unrecognizable shapes or want a tool to manipulate sound in the flow of a performance, Granulator III will take you further into sonic exploration than ever before.
