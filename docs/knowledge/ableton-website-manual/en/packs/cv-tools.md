CV Tools is a Pack of ten devices for Live 11 and Push 3 that generate and receive Pitch, Control, Clock and Trigger CV. Send and modulate CV to and from your modular setup or other CV-based gear and Ableton Live (or Push) using a compatible DC-coupled audio interface. Use Live or Push as the tempo leader or set them to follow your modular system clock.

Even if you don’t own a modular setup, the Rotating Rhythm Generator and CV Utility devices let you adopt a modular-style workflow for Live or your non-modular hardware setup.

## Instruments

### CV Instrument

Control your modular with MIDI. This device features flexible modulation and voicing options, as well as cent-accurate tuning of your oscillators.

![](https://cdn-resources.ableton.com/resources/filer_thumbnails/be/05/be05d100-91a7-4794-bead-81e1783fc3be/cv-instrument.jpg__800x400_q85_crop_subsampling-2_upscale.jpg)

### CV Triggers

Sequence your modular drum modules from inside Live.

![](https://cdn-resources.ableton.com/resources/filer_thumbnails/b6/94/b694d109-86e1-4a88-9516-5bd4676c4ccb/cv-triggers.jpg__800x400_q85_crop_subsampling-2_upscale.jpg)

## Utility

### CV Utility

Control your modular with automation curves from Live. Add, multiply and shift control signals together and process control voltages like audio. You can even use this device if you don’t have any modular gear.

![](https://cdn-resources.ableton.com/resources/filer_thumbnails/65/ce/65ce95a0-cf86-4ce6-b20a-b35e079325f3/cv-utility.jpg__800x400_q85_crop_subsampling-2_upscale.jpg)

## Synchronization

### CV Clock In

Control Live's tempo from your Eurorack system.

![](https://cdn-resources.ableton.com/resources/filer_thumbnails/b2/ec/b2ec23f9-406e-4246-802e-f61672b429f5/cv-clock-in.jpg__800x400_q85_crop_subsampling-2_upscale.jpg)

### CV Clock Out

Send CV clock out to your modular and sync it to Live.

![](https://cdn-resources.ableton.com/resources/filer_thumbnails/0f/66/0f663a44-f6d7-4d1c-870d-e0e1daec8c5b/cv-clock-out.jpg__800x400_q85_crop_subsampling-2_upscale.jpg)

## MIDI Effect

### Rotating Rhythm Generator

Create organic rhythms and experiment with modular-style beat and polyrhythm generation. This device also sends MIDI so you can use it with Drum Racks or other external equipment with a MIDI input.
